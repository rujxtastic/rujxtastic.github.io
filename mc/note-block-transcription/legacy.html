About this tool



This website was made with a one-shot prompt from Deepseek V4 Pro with Deep Thinking - everything except this footer, the .mp3 downloader and the home button was made with a single prompt, on a free model, in 144 seconds. 




I tried asking Claude Sonnet 5.5 Extra, which thought for 45 minutes and burnt all my tokens for the day. I asked Gemini 3.6 Flash and it froze without outputting anything.




I don't like to resort to vibecoding, but it would probably be super boring and take a lot longer than 2 minutes to produce, not to mention really really hard. As a full-time university student I have many, many other things I could be spending my time on.




The only support you can give is visiting my website and starring on GitHub. I don't want to pretend to be owed money or fame for something that I had essentially no hand in.
